# Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 25, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how VeloMap ("the app") is handled by its developer.

Your privacy is important to us. This app is designed to respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible.

## Information Collection and Use

VeloMap does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

The app does not require user accounts, does not track users, and does not collect information such as name, email address, location, or device identifiers.

## Permissions

If the app requests permissions (such as access to storage, camera, or network), these permissions are used **only to provide the core functionality of the app** and the data is processed locally on your device.

No data accessed via permissions is transmitted to external servers.

## Third-Party Services

The app does **not** use third-party services that collect user data (such as analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools).

## Children’s Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
